## Hi there! I'm Abdullah Ajasa 👋

I am a Master's candidate in Systems and Control Engineering at King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals (KFUPM). My research focuses on the intersection of robotics, control theory, optimization, and machine learning, specializing in the robust and adaptive control of dynamical systems.

**🔭 Current Focus**
* **I’m currently working on**: Hardware and software development for hexacopters, and custom ROS packages for real-time Digital Twin simulations.
* **I’m currently researching**: Koopman Operator-Based Control and Learning for Robotic Manipulation.
* **I’m looking to collaborate on**: Open-source robotics, drone control algorithms, and system optimization.
* **How to reach me**: [ajasaabdullah04@gmail.com](mailto:ajasaabdullah04@gmail.com)

**🛠 Tech Stack & Tools**
* **Languages**: C++, Python, SQL, JavaScript, Java, MATLAB/Simulink
* **Robotics & ML**: ROS2, PyTorch, Mujoco, Gazebo, RoboSuite, QGroundControl
* **Hardware & CAD**: Arduino, ESP Series, SolidWorks, Onshape, Autodesk Inventor, Fusion 360

**🚀 Recent Projects**
* Designed and managed power supply and hardware requirements for a Hexacopter drone.
* Developed a custom ROS package for integrating IMU and Motion Capture Data.
* Created ROS control for the Robomaster EP core.
* Implemented PID control for the Quanser Qbot.
